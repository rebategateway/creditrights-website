Received a letter?

Had a letter from us? Here’s what it means.

We wrote to you because you may have had an overdraft, loan or card that you couldn’t really afford. If so, you could be owed back the interest and charges. It takes about a minute to check.

Start your check

Four questions. No documents. No credit check.

Why we wrote

You may have been lent money you couldn’t afford

Banks and lenders are supposed to check you can afford what they lend, and to step in when you’re struggling. When they don’t, they have to put it right: refunding interest and charges, adding interest, and correcting your credit file.

We don’t know anything about your accounts. Our letter is an invitation to check, nothing more. If you’re not interested, you don’t need to do anything.

  • 01In your overdraft most of every month
  • 02Your limit went up while you were struggling
  • 03Borrowing again to pay off borrowing
  • 04Missed payments, and the charges kept coming

What happens next

Three steps, and you only do the first

  1. 01

    Check

  2. 02

    We introduce you

    With your permission, to our legal partner, a firm of solicitors.

  3. 03

    They do the rest

    They check your accounts securely, then complain to your bank or lender for you.
