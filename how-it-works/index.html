How it works

You do the first step. That’s it.

Four questions from you. Our legal partner does the rest. Here’s exactly what happens, who does what, and how long it takes.

02

We introduce you to our legal partner

If you’re eligible, we introduce you to Recoup, a trading style of Clear Legal Limited, a firm of solicitors regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA 469975). They pay us for the introduction. It doesn’t change what you pay.

03

They check your accounts

Recoup asks you to confirm your mobile, then to connect your bank through FCA-regulated open banking. You log in on your bank’s own screen. Access is read-only, covers up to six years, and you can withdraw it at any time. Their solicitors review what it finds.

04

They complain for you

If there’s a case, they send an evidenced complaint to your bank or lender. The lender has eight weeks to reply. If it says no, or doesn’t reply, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, which can take several months.

Your legal partner · weeks to months

05

Things are put right

If your complaint is upheld, the lender refunds interest and charges, adds interest, and corrects your credit file. If you still owe money, the refund may clear or reduce your balance first, with anything left paid to you.

Who does what

Three parts, clearly split

You

  • Answer four questions
  • Agree to be introduced
  • Connect your bank with our partner
  • Sign our partner’s agreement if you go ahead

CreditRights

  • Runs the check
  • Introduces you, with your permission
  • Never charges you
  • FCA authorised, FRN 830182

Recoup

  • Checks your accounts
  • Makes the complaint
  • Takes it to the Financial Ombudsman if needed
  • Charges a capped fee, only if you win

What you’ll need

  • A minute for the check.
  • Your online banking login, when our partner asks you to connect your account. You log in on your bank’s own screen, so you never share it.
  • Nothing else. No statements or paperwork.

What it costs

The check is free and CreditRights never charges you. If you go ahead, our legal partner charges a fee only if your claim succeeds, capped by the regulator.
