Short answers to what people ask most.

All questionsHow it works

Struggling with debt now? MoneyHelper offers free, impartial debt advice.

Can I claim for my bank overdraft?

You could, if you relied on it month after month or your limit went up while you were struggling.

MoreOverdraft claims explained
Can I claim if I’ve paid it off?

Yes. Repaid and closed accounts can still be claimed for, subject to time limits.

Will claiming affect my credit file?

Checking doesn’t search your credit file. If a claim is upheld, wrong markers can be removed.
