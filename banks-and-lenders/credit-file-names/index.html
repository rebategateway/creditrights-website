Questions

Why does my credit file show a company name I don’t recognise?

Lenders often report under their legal company name rather than the brand you borrowed from. For example, 118 118 Money is provided by Madison CF UK Limited, QuidMarket is provided by Propel Holdings (UK) Limited (formerly Stagemount Limited), Aqua is provided by NewDay Ltd.

Can I claim if I only know the company name?

Yes. Find the name in the list on this page to see which brand it is, then use that lender’s page or our check. The complaint goes to the company that provided the credit.

One company covers several brands. Do I complain to each?

No. Where several brands belong to one company, one complaint to that company can cover all of them.

What if the company on my file is a debt collector?

Then the debt was probably sold. You can still complain about the lending to the original lender.
