Bank of Scotland overdraft refund claims

The short answer

If you relied on your Bank of Scotland overdraft month after month, or your limit went up while you were struggling, you could be owed back the interest and charges. Complaints go to Bank of Scotland first, then to the Financial Ombudsman.

Who are Bank of Scotland?

Bank of Scotland is Lloyds Banking Group’s brand in Scotland, provided by Bank of Scotland plc. Unlike Halifax, it is not moving to the Lloyds brand.

Its Classic account overdraft is 29.9% EAR variable (representative), with each customer’s rate set individually (checked October 2026).

Part of Lloyds Banking Group. Products: current accounts, overdrafts, credit cards, loans.

Can I claim against Bank of Scotland?

You could, if any of these sound familiar:

  1. 01In your overdraft most of every month
  2. 02Wages in, straight back into the red
  3. 03Your limit went up while you were maxed out
  4. 04Charges kept coming, and nobody got in touch

Limit increases count too. Each one was a lending decision, so each can be looked at.

Ombudsman decisions about Bank of Scotland

Published Financial Ombudsman decisions show how complaints like these are judged. Where a complaint is upheld, it is often only from a certain date or for certain lending. Every complaint is decided on its own facts, so these are examples, not a guide to what you’ll get.

Bank of Scotland overdraft questions

Can I claim if I’m still overdrawn?

Yes. If the complaint is upheld, the refund may reduce your balance rather than being paid to you in cash.

Will my overdraft be taken away?

It can be. If a complaint is upheld, the bank may reduce or remove the overdraft and agree an affordable way to repay what’s left.

How long does Bank of Scotland have to reply?

Eight weeks. If Bank of Scotland hasn’t sent a final response by then, or you’re unhappy with it, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, normally within six months of the final response.

How far back can I claim?

Usually six years from the lending, or three years from when you realised you had cause to complain, whichever is later. Where an account is still running, the ombudsman has sometimes looked back further.

Complain to Bank of Scotland yourself

You don’t need a claims company. You can complain to Bank of Scotland directly using its complaints page. If you’re unhappy with the answer, or it hasn’t replied within eight weeks, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman. Both are free.
