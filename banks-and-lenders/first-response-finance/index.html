First Response Finance car finance refund claims

The short answer

If First Response Finance gave you car finance when the monthly payments weren’t affordable, you could be owed back the interest and charges. This is separate from car finance commission claims. Complaints go to First Response Finance first, then to the Financial Ombudsman.

Who are First Response Finance?

First Response Finance Limited is a Nottingham-based motor finance lender, registered office 5 Regan Way, Chetwynd Business Park, Chilwell. It lends mainly to non-prime customers through dealers and offers hire purchase for cars, motorbikes and vans. It is authorised by the FCA to enter into regulated credit agreements as a lender.

Products: car finance (hire purchase).

Can I claim against First Response Finance?

You could, if any of these sound familiar:

  1. 01The monthly payments took a big share of your income
  2. 02You fell behind soon after taking the finance
  3. 03You already had missed payments or defaults
  4. 04You were already juggling other credit when you took it

Affordability complaints are about whether you could afford the payments. Commission complaints are a different issue.

Ombudsman decisions about First Response Finance

Published Financial Ombudsman decisions show how complaints like these are judged. Where a complaint is upheld, it is often only from a certain date or for certain lending. Every complaint is decided on its own facts, so these are examples, not a guide to what you’ll get.

  • Upheld

    The complaint was about a September 2021 used-car hire purchase agreement (£7,995 credit, 48 payments of £292.87). First Response had to end the agreement with nothing more to pay, transfer ownership of the car to the customers, refund anything above the £8,495 cash price with interest, and remove adverse credit file entries.

    Read decision DRN-6060220
  • Upheld

    The complaint was about a used-car hire purchase agreement for £10,000 of credit over 61 months. Mrs L pays only the £12,389.99 cash price, with no interest or charges. First Response refunds anything above that with interest and removes adverse credit file entries.

    Read decision DRN-5764052
  • Not upheld

    The complaint was about a March 2018 used-car hire purchase agreement. The ombudsman found the decision to lend was not unfair and ordered no redress.

    Read decision DRN-5501889

First Response Finance car finance questions

Is this the same as a car finance commission claim?

No. Commission claims are about how the dealer was paid. Affordability claims are about whether you could afford the payments. You may be able to make both.

Can I claim if the car was returned or repossessed?

Yes. Ended agreements can still be looked at, subject to time limits.

How long does First Response Finance have to reply?

Eight weeks. If First Response Finance hasn’t sent a final response by then, or you’re unhappy with it, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, normally within six months of the final response.

How far back can I claim?

Usually six years from the lending, or three years from when you realised you had cause to complain, whichever is later. Where an account is still running, the ombudsman has sometimes looked back further.

How to complain to First Response Finance

You don’t need a claims company. You can complain to First Response Finance directly using its complaints page. If you’re unhappy with the answer, or it hasn’t replied within eight weeks, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman. Both are free.
