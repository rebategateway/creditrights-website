PayPal Pay in 3 complaints

The short answer

Buy now pay later plans became regulated on 15 July 2026. If PayPal Pay in 3 agreed plans from that date that you couldn’t afford, you can complain about them. Complaints go to PayPal Pay in 3 first, then to the Financial Ombudsman.

Regulated from 15 July 2026. The FCA began regulating buy now pay later (deferred payment credit) on 15 July 2026. PayPal describes Pay in 3 as a regulated deferred payment credit agreement, and its current agreement is dated 15 July 2026. Under the FCA’s rules, agreements taken out before 15 July 2026 remain unregulated.

Who are PayPal Pay in 3?

PayPal Pay in 3 splits an online purchase into three interest-free payments: one at checkout and two more monthly. It is provided by PayPal UK Ltd, registered office 76 Upper Ground, London SE1 9PZ, which holds FCA consumer credit authorisation under firm reference 996405. PayPal says it may run a soft credit check, which does not affect the customer’s credit score.

PayPal launched Pay in 3 in the UK in October 2020 for purchases between £45 and £2,000; it now appears for eligible baskets between £20 and £3,000. At launch PayPal said it had more than 24 million UK users.

Part of PayPal. Products: deferred payment credit (three interest-free instalments).

Can I claim against PayPal Pay in 3?

You could, if any of these sound familiar:

  1. 01You had several plans running at once
  2. 02You used plans for food, bills or everyday shopping
  3. 03You were charged for missed payments
  4. 04You were already struggling with other debts

Only plans taken out from 15 July 2026 are covered. Older plans stay unregulated, but other credit you had may still be worth checking.

The ombudsman’s usual approach to unaffordable lending is to remove charges added, refund any overpayment with interest and remove negative entries from your credit file. Because most plans are interest-free, the main gains are charges and your credit file.

PayPal Pay in 3 pay later plan questions

Can I complain about older PayPal Pay in 3 plans?

Not about affordability if they were interest-free plans taken out before 15 July 2026, because they weren’t regulated. Other credit you had may still be worth checking.

Do I need to list every plan?

Say which plans you want reviewed. PayPal Pay in 3 holds the details of each plan and payment.

How long does PayPal Pay in 3 have to reply?

Eight weeks. If PayPal Pay in 3 hasn’t sent a final response by then, or you’re unhappy with it, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, normally within six months of the final response.

How far back can I claim?

Usually six years from the lending, or three years from when you realised you had cause to complain, whichever is later. Where an account is still running, the ombudsman has sometimes looked back further.

How to complain to PayPal Pay in 3

You don’t need a claims company. You can complain to PayPal Pay in 3 directly using its complaints page. If you’re unhappy with the answer, or it hasn’t replied within eight weeks, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman. Both are free.
