Klarna pay later complaints

The short answer

Buy now pay later plans became regulated on 15 July 2026. If Klarna agreed plans from that date that you couldn’t afford, you can complain about them. Complaints go to Klarna first, then to the Financial Ombudsman.

Pay in 3 and Pay in 30 days regulated from 15 July 2026. The FCA started regulating interest-free buy now, pay later on 15 July 2026. Pay in 3 and Pay in 30 days plans taken out from that date are covered, including affordability checks and the right to go to the Financial Ombudsman. Plans taken out before then remain unregulated. Klarna Financing was already a regulated credit agreement before that date.

Who are Klarna?

Klarna Financial Services UK Limited is the UK company through which Klarna provides credit. Its registered office is 10 York Road, London, and it is authorised and regulated by the FCA for consumer credit. It is owned by Klarna Bank AB, a Swedish bank. Before the UK company was authorised in 2023, Klarna Bank AB (publ) provided Klarna’s regulated credit in the UK, and older ombudsman decisions name it as the lender.

Klarna offers Pay in 3, Pay in 30 days and Financing, which spreads larger purchases over 6 to 24 months and can be interest-free or charge interest. Klarna said in May 2025 that it had passed 11 million UK customers and that the UK was its third-largest market.

Part of Klarna Bank AB. Products: pay in 3, Pay in 30 days, Financing (fixed-term instalment credit), Klarna Credit Card.

Can I claim against Klarna?

You could, if any of these sound familiar:

  1. 01You had several plans running at once
  2. 02You used plans for food, bills or everyday shopping
  3. 03You were charged for missed payments
  4. 04You were already struggling with other debts

Only plans taken out from 15 July 2026 are covered. Older plans stay unregulated, but other credit you had may still be worth checking.

The ombudsman’s usual approach to unaffordable lending is to remove charges added, refund any overpayment with interest and remove negative entries from your credit file. Because most plans are interest-free, the main gains are charges and your credit file.

Ombudsman decisions about Klarna

Published Financial Ombudsman decisions show how complaints like these are judged. Where a complaint is upheld, it is often only from a certain date or for certain lending. Every complaint is decided on its own facts, so these are examples, not a guide to what you’ll get.

  • Not upheld

    Mr E complained that Klarna Financial Services UK irresponsibly gave him a £1,020 point of sale loan he could not afford, and then reported a default. The ombudsman found Klarna’s checks were reasonable and proportionate, showing enough disposable income and no defaults, and that the default was reported accurately. No redress was ordered.

    Read decision DRN-5764204
  • Not upheld

    Mr D used Klarna’s ’Pay Over 6 Months’ running-account credit for a £439.99 purchase in June 2020. The ombudsman found Klarna Bank AB’s checks, including his declared £60,000 income and a credit search showing no defaults or arrears, were proportionate and raised no concerns. No redress was ordered.

    Read decision DRN-5931795
  • Not upheld

    Miss M took a £5,000 Klarna loan to fund a purchase in May 2023, repayable at £130.64 a month. The ombudsman found Klarna Bank AB’s checks were proportionate and it was reasonable to accept her declared £18,000 income without verifying it. No redress was ordered.

    Read decision DRN-5570382

Klarna pay later plan questions

Can I complain about older Klarna plans?

Not about affordability if they were interest-free plans taken out before 15 July 2026, because they weren’t regulated. Other credit you had may still be worth checking.

Do I need to list every plan?

Say which plans you want reviewed. Klarna holds the details of each plan and payment.

How long does Klarna have to reply?

Eight weeks. If Klarna hasn’t sent a final response by then, or you’re unhappy with it, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, normally within six months of the final response.

How far back can I claim?

Usually six years from the lending, or three years from when you realised you had cause to complain, whichever is later. Where an account is still running, the ombudsman has sometimes looked back further.

Which Klarna plans can I complain about to the Financial Ombudsman?

Klarna Financing agreements, which were regulated credit before July 2026, and Pay in 3 or Pay in 30 days plans taken out on or after 15 July 2026. Pay in 3 and Pay in 30 days plans taken out before 15 July 2026 are unregulated, and Klarna says complaints about them are outside the ombudsman’s remit. Complain to Klarna first; if you are unhappy with its final response, you can go to the ombudsman.

How to complain to Klarna

You don’t need a claims company. You can complain to Klarna directly using its complaints page. If you’re unhappy with the answer, or it hasn’t replied within eight weeks, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman. Both are free.
