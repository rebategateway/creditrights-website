Drafty loan refund claims

The short answer

If Drafty lent to you when the repayments weren’t affordable, or kept lending when you were already struggling, you could be owed back the interest and charges. Complaints go to Drafty first, then to the Financial Ombudsman.

Who are Drafty?

Drafty is run by GAIN Credit LLC, the company behind Lending Stream. It offers a running line of credit from £50 to £3,000, and fixed-term loans of £1,000 to £3,000.

With a line of credit, the question is often when the lender should have noticed you were borrowing to repay borrowing.

Part of GAIN Credit. Products: line of credit and fixed-term loans.

Can I claim against Drafty?

You could, if any of these sound familiar:

  1. 01You borrowed again soon after paying off a loan
  2. 02You had several loans running at once
  3. 03You borrowed to pay bills or other debts
  4. 04You already had missed payments or defaults

Each loan is looked at separately. Even if the first was fine, later ones may not have been.

Ombudsman decisions about Drafty

Published Financial Ombudsman decisions show how complaints like these are judged. Where a complaint is upheld, it is often only from a certain date or for certain lending. Every complaint is decided on its own facts, so these are examples, not a guide to what you’ll get.

Drafty loan questions

Can I claim if I’ve paid the loan off?

Yes. Repaid loans can still be looked at, subject to time limits.

What if my loan was sold to a debt collector?

You still complain to Drafty, the lender that made the decision to lend.

How long does Drafty have to reply?

Eight weeks. If Drafty hasn’t sent a final response by then, or you’re unhappy with it, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, normally within six months of the final response.

How far back can I claim?

Usually six years from the lending, or three years from when you realised you had cause to complain, whichever is later. Where an account is still running, the ombudsman has sometimes looked back further.

Complain to Drafty yourself

You don’t need a claims company. You can complain to Drafty directly using its complaints page. If you’re unhappy with the answer, or it hasn’t replied within eight weeks, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman. Both are free.
