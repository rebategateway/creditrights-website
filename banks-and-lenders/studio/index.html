Studio account refund claims

The short answer

If Studio kept raising your account limit while you were struggling to pay, you could be owed back the interest and charges. Complaints go to Studio first, then to the Financial Ombudsman.

Where Studio Pay complaints go. Studio says complaints about lending on a Studio Pay account, the sale of the balance or account closure should be emailed to studiopaycomplaints@frasers.plus. This applies even if the account is now handled by Link Financial. Accounts that moved to Frasers Plus use the Frasers Plus support form.

Who are Studio?

Studio’s credit accounts are provided by Frasers Group Financial Services Limited. The company was called Studio Retail Limited until 31 May 2023 and Express Gifts Limited until January 2019. Frasers Group bought Studio out of administration in February 2022. The company is based at Clayton Business Park, Accrington. It now provides the Frasers Plus credit product, and Studio’s retail arm describes itself as a credit introducer, not a lender.

Ombudsman decisions published after the sale, including decisions against both Studio Retail Limited and Frasers Group Financial Services Limited, show that the company still answers complaints about accounts opened before 2022.

Part of Frasers Group. Products: frasers Plus credit account; formerly Studio Pay accounts.

Can I claim against Studio?

You could, if any of these sound familiar:

  1. 01Your limit went up while you were only making minimum payments
  2. 02Your balance was always close to the limit
  3. 03You were using it for essentials you couldn’t otherwise afford
  4. 04You were already struggling with other debts

Limit increases count too. Each one was a lending decision, so each can be looked at.

Ombudsman decisions about Studio

Published Financial Ombudsman decisions show how complaints like these are judged. Where a complaint is upheld, it is often only from a certain date or for certain lending. Every complaint is decided on its own facts, so these are examples, not a guide to what you’ll get.

  • Upheld

    A Studio account was opened in June 2019 with a £200 limit, which was raised to £600 in November 2019. The ombudsman found Studio Retail Limited acted unfairly in extending credit and ordered a refund of all interest and charges.

    Read decision DRN-4024273
  • Partly upheld

    A Studio account opened in about November 2018 with a £150 limit had its limit raised several times, to £1,600 by November 2020. The ombudsman found the later increases were irresponsible but the earlier ones were not, and ordered the account to be reworked.

    Read decision DRN-6046390
  • Not upheld

    A Studio account was opened in December 2020 with a £375 limit, which was raised to £600 by May 2021. The ombudsman found the checks were proportionate and the lending decisions were reasonable.

    Read decision DRN-4876341

Studio account questions

Can I claim for limit increases?

Yes. Each increase was a separate lending decision, so Studio should have checked it was affordable each time.

Will my account be suspended?

Some lenders suspend the account while they look into an affordability complaint. Ask the lender if you’re unsure.

How long does Studio have to reply?

Eight weeks. If Studio hasn’t sent a final response by then, or you’re unhappy with it, the complaint can go to the Financial Ombudsman, normally within six months of the final response.

How far back can I claim?

Usually six years from the lending, or three years from when you realised you had cause to complain, whichever is later. Where an account is still running, the ombudsman has sometimes looked back further.

How to complain to Studio

You don’t need a claims company. You can complain to Studio directly using its complaints page. If you’re unhappy with the answer, or it hasn’t replied within eight weeks, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman. Both are free.
